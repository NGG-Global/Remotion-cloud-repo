# Alcatraz documentary: sources and credits

Every picture in `AlcatrazDocumentary` comes from a public archive and is listed here with the record it was taken from. The narration (`audio/narration.mp3`) was supplied by the production.

Licences are given as the archive states them. Most material is a U.S. federal government work or otherwise in the public domain. A few items carry a Creative Commons licence or no stated licence; the production has confirmed that rights and credits for all third-party material are cleared, but **check the terms for the items marked below before publishing**.

The bay map is drawn from OpenStreetMap coastline data, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

## Items to check before publishing

- `gangster` clips: PeriscopeFilm, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 as stated on the Internet Archive item. The prohibition footage inside it is older newsreel material.
- `b24-aerial`: Flickr upload, CC BY-NC-SA 2.0.
- `heli` clips: California Revealed / Center for Sacramento History; no licence stated on the item.
- `ferry-2020` and the other Library of Congress items in the New York World-Telegram & Sun collection: the Library notes that publication may be restricted for some items in that collection.
- The NPS images are from Golden Gate National Recreation Area web pages; NPS-produced material is public domain, but some park archive items are credited to other photographers.
