Verify them before any use outside this project, and keep this list with any published copy of the film: the CC BY and CC BY-SA photographs below require attribution. The video credits them on screen when they appear and lists the archives in its end card; paste this list into the YouTube description.

## Other material

- Fonts: Frank Ruhl Libre, Playfair Display and Rubik (SIL OFL 1.1, already in `public/fonts`); an eleven-glyph subset of Noto Serif JP for the Japanese words (SIL OFL 1.1, `public/fonts/NotoSerifJP-LICENSE.txt`).
- Score and sound effects: synthesised by `tools/hiroshima/score.mjs`. No samples, no third-party music.
- Narration: the three supplied files, `public/hiroshima/audio/narration-{1,2,3}.mp3`, byte-identical to the uploads.
