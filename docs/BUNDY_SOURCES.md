# Faces of Evil: Ted Bundy — sources and licences

Every picture in `TedBundy` is listed here. Licence notes are as recorded on each source page when the material was downloaded (November 2026). Verify them before any use outside this project, and keep this list with any published copy of the film: the CC BY photograph below requires attribution. The video credits it on screen and lists the archives in its end card; paste this list into the YouTube description.

Generated by `tools/bundy/sources-doc.mjs`.

## Moving pictures

Period atmosphere only: all are U.S. government films, none shows Bundy, the victims or the places of the story, and the video credits each one on screen whenever it appears. A 1978 home movie from Prelinger Archives used in an early cut was removed because its item page states no licence and Prelinger's own FAQ says many of its films are not in the public domain. Downloaded from the Internet Archive into `.cache/raw-bundy`, cut and graded into `public/bundy/clips` by `tools/hiroshima/cut-clips.mjs bundy`.
