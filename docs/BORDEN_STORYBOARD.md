# Lizzie Borden storyboard

Shot list for `LizzieBorden`, cut to `public/audio/lizzie-narration.mp3` (948.19 s, 15:48).

Times are seconds into that file. Each row is one shot in `SHOTS` (`src/borden/data/timeline.ts`); the shot's component is in `src/borden/shots/`. "In" is how the shot starts: a cut, a dissolve (**diss**) or a fade up from black (**black**). Every cut sits in a measured pause of the voice (the shortest quiet stretch around a cut is 0.20 s, the median 0.67 s).

Only the 14 labels in `LABELS` are on-screen type. Everything else that can be read is part of the picture: a masthead, a calendar, a name on a step, a word on a slate.

Nothing violent is shown. No body is drawn; the most the picture allows itself is a hem and a shoe beyond a bed, and a dark shape under it. The killer, whoever it was, is only ever a silhouette.
