# Gacy storyboard

Visual beats for `GacyDocumentary`, cut to `ElevenLabs_John_Wayne_Gacy_מאחורי_הסיוט.mp3` (645.3s).

Times are seconds into that file. A beat is a change of framing, action, or reveal inside a location — not a new title card. Text on screen stays under a few words.

The clown appears in the opening pass, in the Pogo section, and once at the end, when the narration is actually about the costume. Everywhere else Gacy is an ordinary heavyset man.

## Opening — the ordinary night (0:00–1:08)

### 0:00–0:09 · Series line, then the street

- Narration: the episode name, and that the famous part of the story is the least important.
- Location: a Chicago suburb at night.
- Characters: none in the foreground yet. Window silhouettes inside one lit house.
- Framing: wide. Slow push from the end of the street toward the lit house.
- Action: a distant car crosses. Trees shift. Lamps glow.
- Environment: ranch houses, lawns, a road, a moon.
- Props: street lamps, a passing sedan.
- Animation: camera push, window light, star flicker, car travel.
- Transition: the push continues; we do not cut to a title.

### 0:09–0:18 · The lit house

- Narration: Gacy really did dress as a clown named Pogo, at parties and community events.
- Location: the same street, closer.
- Characters: two small silhouettes in the window.
- Framing: medium-wide on the house, warm windows dominant.
- Action: the camera keeps drifting forward.
- Environment: night suburb.
- Props: porch, lit windows.
- Animation: slow zoom, lamp flicker.
- Transition: lateral drift. A hanging costume starts to enter from the side.

### 0:18–0:30 · Passing the costume

- Narration: white makeup, a red nose, the whole package — and that movies overstated it.
- Location: the street, then a gathering just past the houses.
- Characters: Gacy in ordinary clothes, talking; two neighbors.
- Framing: the camera slides sideways. A Pogo costume on a stand crosses the foreground and leaves.
- Action: Gacy gestures as if mid-conversation. Neighbors idle and answer.
- Environment: lawn, another house, a lamp.
- Props: the hanging costume (ruff, white face, red suit), not a monster.
- Animation: foreground costume moves faster than the people (parallax).
- Transition: the slide continues into work, without a fade.

### 0:30–0:42 · The costume was the man

- Narration: he did not need a disguise. Businessman, neighbor, employer.
- Location: a small construction stretch beside the party.
- Characters: Gacy in work clothes, pointing; two young workers, one walking, one carrying.
- Framing: medium, still moving right.
- Action: Gacy directs. A worker walks the site.
- Environment: overcast-to-night job site.
- Props: cement mixer turning, stacked lumber, scaffold poles.
- Animation: mixer spin, walk cycle, camera drift.
- Transition: the camera whips back toward the first house and the frame darkens.

### 0:42–0:57 · Under the floor

- Narration: for years, under the house, something nobody around him could imagine.
- Location: beneath the lit house.
- Characters: none. The horror is the space.
- Framing: the lawn fills the frame, then we are in a low dirt crawl space.
- Action: the camera drops.
- Environment: narrow darkness, pipes suggested by the cut of the soil.
- Props: three dark trenches in the dirt. No remains.
- Animation: motivated dip through black, then a slow move across the soil.
- Transition: pull back out.

### 0:57–1:08 · The name

- Narration: this is his story, and almost by accident why clowns scare people.
- Location: the suburb again, wider.
- Characters: distant, back in the street we left.
- Framing: wide, slightly high, resting on the house.
- Action: the street is quiet again.
- Environment: the same night.
- Props: houses, lamps.
- Animation: slow zoom out. The name fades in once.
- On-screen text: ג'ון וויין גייסי
- Transition: hard cut on the narration's turn to 1942. The dusk skyline replaces the street.
