A 2:48 capability showreel for HyperFrames. Teach what the platform can actually
make: timed HTML, GSAP, nested compositions, catalog blocks (charts, maps,
flowcharts, code, overlays), kinetic captions, Three.js depth, variables, and a
mixed audio bed. Dark / mint, developer audience, no product-site capture.
